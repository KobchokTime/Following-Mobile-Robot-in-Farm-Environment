# E7 — which part of the target selection helps? (twin)

- **Date:** 2026-10-24
- **Code:** `integration-hub` after the Arena 3 merge (`efda1b0`): `TargetGate` and give-way in
  `Image/sync_fusion_node.py`.
- **Question:** the thesis claims that appearance (clothing colour) and probabilistic fusion beat
  YOLO or LiDAR alone (expected benefit 2), but it has no comparison. This experiment switches each
  part off in turn. **Simulation only.**

## Protocol

- **Scenarios:** the 5 identity scenarios (`crossing`, `side_by_side`, `side_by_side_same`,
  `head_on`, `stand_aside`) on the `fixed` and `diff_drive` bridges, seeds 0, 1 and 2. That is 30 runs
  per condition.
- **Conditions:** each part is switched off with a ROS parameter only (`run_scenario --ros-param`).
  The code is identical.

  | Condition | Parameter |
  |---|---|
  | full | — |
  | no colour | `use_chroma:=false` |
  | no give-way | `path_half_width_m:=0.0` |
  | no gate (the old pipeline) | `target_gate:=false`. This also switches off the give-way, which only runs for detections the gate rejects (Codex, T9). |

- **Pass:** the same as Arena 3: target in view at the end, within 1.5 m, no contact, and the target
  in view for at least 90% of the run.

Raw summaries: `e7_ablation_2026-10-24.json`.

## Results

| Condition | Runs passed | Contacts | Closest other person | Where it fails |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| full | **30 / 30** | 0 | 0.52 m | — |
| no colour | 27 / 30 | 0 | 0.50 m | `stand_aside`/fixed, seeds 1 and 2: final gap 6.1 m, target visible 23%; `crossing`/diff_drive, seed 0: target visible 70% |
| no give-way | 28 / 30 | **2** | 0.39 m | `crossing`/diff_drive, seeds 0 and 1: contact with the crossing person (0.39 m) |
| no gate (and so no give-way) | 12 / 30 | **8** | 0.43 m | `side_by_side` 0/6 (3 contacts), `side_by_side_same` 0/6 (5 contacts), `stand_aside` 0/6 |

## What it shows

1. **Data association is the main effect.**
   - "No gate" switches off the gate and the give-way together. So the gate's own effect is the
     comparison between "no give-way" (gate on, give-way off: 28/30) and "no gate" (both off: 12/30).
   - That is a net difference of +16 runs:
     - `side_by_side`, `side_by_side_same` and `stand_aside` go from 0/6 to 6/6 each, a gain of 18.
     - `crossing` goes the other way, from 6/6 to 4/6, a loss of 2. Once the gate ignores the crossing
       person, only the give-way keeps the robot clear of them.
   - The old pipeline passes only `crossing` and `head_on`, where the other person never looks like
     the target. That is the thesis's problem statement 3, and the old pipeline does not solve it.
   - The 30 vs 12 comparison is the whole change against the old pipeline, not the gate alone.
     *(Corrected after Codex's T9 review.)*
2. **Colour is a tie-breaker, and it matters.**
   - In `stand_aside` the bystander stands about 12° and 0.25 m from where the target is predicted.
     On geometry alone the cost is about 8.6, just inside the gate (9).
   - In 2 of 6 runs the track swaps to the bystander. The bystander is still, so the robot stops and
     the target walks away.
   - With colour, the difference (blue against red, even in shade) pushes the cost over the gate.
   - This is the first evidence in this project that the thesis's colour feature helps: 27 → 30.
   - In `side_by_side_same` (the same clothes) geometry alone already passes 6/6, so colour is not
     needed there.
3. **Ignoring other people is not enough.** Give-way's own effect is full against "no give-way":
   +2 runs and 2 fewer contacts. Without the give-way, a person crossing between the
   robot and the target is ignored while they hide the target. This caused 2 contacts. Target
   selection and keeping clear of others are separate jobs.

## Limits

- The colour model is synthetic (`docs/thesis_review_2026-10-22.md`, C2/C3). The colour result needs
  E4 and E5 on the robot before the thesis can state it.
- The gate's σ values, the corridor (0.7 m) and the margin (0.4 m) were tuned in this twin. A
  different bystander position, closer than the gate's σ, could still swap even with colour on.
- There is 1 run per seed per bridge, so 6 runs per scenario per condition. "2 of 6" is a signal,
  not a rate.
